Privacy Policy for DoseKoper

We explain, in plain language, what information DoseKoper collects, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and the choices you have.

Effective date: October 15, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 15, 2026

The short version. DoseKoper is built to keep your personal information to a minimum. We only collect the data we genuinely need to run the app and improve it. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

1. Overview & Summary

This Privacy Policy describes how DoseKoper ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the DoseKoper mobile application and any related services (collectively, the "App"). It applies to all users of the App, anywhere in the world, and is intended to comply with major privacy frameworks including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended, and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where it applies.

If you are a resident of the United States, you may have additional rights under your state's privacy law. These are described in Section 11.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways: information you give us directly, information the App generates automatically, and information from third parties.

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password when you create an account.
  • Profile information: optional details such as your profile photo, display name, or preferences.
  • User content: anything you enter, upload, save, or share through the App, including messages, notes, files, images, or settings.
  • Communications: information you include when you contact us for support, and records of that correspondence.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device information: device model, operating system and version, language, time zone, and device identifiers.
  • Usage information: which features you use, session length, crash reports, performance data, and interaction events.
  • Network information: approximate location (derived from IP address), and connection type.

2.3 Information from third parties

We may receive information from service providers (for example, payment processors, analytics providers, or authentication services) when you connect a third-party account or complete a transaction. We only use this information for the purposes described in this policy.

Category of dataPurpose & legal basis
Account & profile data To create and manage your account; performance of our contract with you.
User content To provide the features you request and sync your information across devices; performance of our contract with you.
Device & usage data To operate, secure, and improve the App; our legitimate interests in maintaining and developing our services.
Support communications To respond to your requests and keep records; our legitimate interests and, where relevant, your consent.
Payment information To process subscriptions and purchases; performance of our contract with you. We do not store full payment card numbers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  1. Provide, operate, and maintain the App and its features;
  2. Create and manage your account and authenticate your identity;
  3. Process transactions and send related confirmations;
  4. Respond to your questions, requests, and support needs;
  5. Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
  6. Understand how the App is used so we can improve it;
  7. Send service-related notices (for example, about changes to the App or this policy);
  8. Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use your personal information to target you with third-party advertising, and we do not sell it.

4. How We Share Information

We only share information with the categories of recipients listed below, and only as needed for the purposes described in this policy.

  • Service providers: companies that help us operate the App, such as hosting, cloud storage, analytics, customer support, and payment processing. They are contractually required to use your information only on our behalf.
  • Legal and safety purposes: when we believe disclosure is necessary to comply with a law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable government request; to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or others; or to detect and address fraud or security issues.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, sale, acquisition, or similar corporate transaction, in which case we will notify you before your information is transferred under a different privacy policy.
  • With your consent: in any other circumstance where you have given us specific permission.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

The App may use local storage, cookies, SDKs, and similar technologies to remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and understand usage patterns. You can control or disable some of these technologies through your device or browser settings, but doing so may affect certain features of the App.

We do not use cross-app advertising identifiers for behavioral advertising, and we do not permit third parties to use the App to collect information about you for their own advertising purposes.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information from loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. These include encryption in transit, access controls, and regular security reviews.

No method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we work hard to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as long as needed to provide you with the App. We may also retain certain information as required to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce our agreements.

When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except for the limited information we are required to retain by law.

8. International Data Transfers

DoseKoper may be operated from, and process information in, countries other than the one in which you reside. When we transfer personal information across borders, we use appropriate safeguards recognized under applicable law, such as standard contractual clauses, to protect your information.

By using the App, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other jurisdictions.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

Residents of jurisdictions that set a higher minimum age should refrain from using the App if they are below that minimum age.

10. Your Choices & Rights

You can access, update, or correct most of your information directly within the App's settings. You can also contact us to exercise any of the rights described below.

  • Access and portability: request a copy of the personal information we hold about you in a commonly used format.
  • Correction: ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete personal information we hold about you, subject to certain legal exceptions.
  • Objection and restriction: object to or request that we limit our use of your information.
  • Withdraw consent: where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Opt out of "sales" or "sharing": U.S. residents may opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. DoseKoper does not sell personal information and does not share it for such advertising.

If you believe we have not handled your information properly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
